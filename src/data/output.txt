### Revised Sample Share Repurchase Rights Provisions

**Article X: Share Repurchase Rights**

**Section 1: Definition of Material Breach**  
For the purposes of this Agreement, a "Material Breach" shall include, but not be limited to, any of the following events:  
   a. A failure to comply with any material term or condition of this Agreement;  
   b. Any act of fraud, dishonesty, or willful misconduct by the shareholder;  
   c. A violation of any applicable laws or regulations that materially affects the company or its operations;  
   d. Any failure to fulfill obligations related to the payment of capital contributions or other financial commitments;  
   e. Any other act or omission that materially harms the interests of the Company or its shareholders.

**Section 2: Right to Repurchase**  
   a. In the event that a shareholder commits a Material Breach, the Company shall have the right, but not the obligation, to repurchase all or any portion of the shares held by the breaching shareholder (the "Repurchase Right").  
   b. Additionally, any non-breaching shareholder(s) shall have the right to repurchase the shares of the breaching shareholder on the same terms and conditions as the Company.

**Section 3: Notice of Material Breach**  
   a. Upon the occurrence of a Material Breach, the Company or any non-breaching shareholder shall provide written notice to the breaching shareholder (the "Breach Notice") detailing the nature of the Material Breach.  
   b. The breaching shareholder shall have thirty (30) days from the date of receipt of the Breach Notice to cure the Material Breach, if it is capable of being cured.  

**Section 4: Exercise of Repurchase Right**  
   a. If the Material Breach is not cured within the thirty (30) day period, the Company or any non-breaching shareholder may exercise the Repurchase Right by providing written notice to the breaching shareholder (the "Repurchase Notice").  
   b. The Repurchase Notice shall specify the number of shares to be repurchased and the purchase price, which shall be determined as follows:  
      i. The fair market value of the shares as determined by an independent appraiser selected by the Company, or  
      ii. If agreed upon by the parties, a predetermined formula set forth in this Agreement.

**Section 5: Closing of Repurchase**  
   a. The closing of the repurchase shall occur within thirty (30) days after the delivery of the Repurchase Notice, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.  
   b. At the closing, the breaching shareholder shall deliver the shares to the Company or the non-breaching shareholder, and the Company or the non-breaching shareholder shall pay the purchase price in cash or other agreed-upon consideration.

**Section 6: Waiver of Rights**  
   a. The failure of the Company or any non-breaching shareholder to exercise the Repurchase Right upon the occurrence of a Material Breach shall not be deemed a waiver of such right in the future or with respect to any subsequent Material Breach.

**Section 7: Governing Law**  
   a. This Article X shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of [insert state].
